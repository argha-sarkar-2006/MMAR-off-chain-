Values

The Principles We Build By

Radical Privacy

Your data is sovereign. Documents ingested into 3rd-Route reside in your local SQLite files. No telemetry, no covert training sets, no cloud surveillance.

Interoperable Modularity

Every pipeline stage is decoupled. Want to swap Gemini with a local Llama model? Simply change your configuration without rewriting code.

Speed Without Bloat

We avoid heavyweight vector databases that consume gigabytes of RAM. SQLite FTS5 provides lightning-fast keyword and semantic retrieval with zero memory overhead.
